Battle_PVP adaptation:
- Imported as Unity Humanoid and retargeted to Player.prefab.
- Sampled the prone pose at 0.15 seconds, removed horizontal displacement,
  and aligned it to the player's forward direction.
- Combined with existing standing and Quaternius kneeling poses for a
  three-second enter / brace / stand sequence (DEF_Fortify.anim).
- Build reproducibly with FortifyMotionInstaller.Apply().

2026-10-05 update:
