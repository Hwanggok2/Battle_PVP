GrapplingHook.svg is the original free vector artwork. GrapplingHook.obj adapts
its hook outline into a solid 3D mesh, retaining the eyelet and omitting the rope.
Adaptation: Tools/SkillExpansion/build_hook_mesh.py. This is an extruded adaptation,
not an original third-party 3D model. No script from the source archive is used.
