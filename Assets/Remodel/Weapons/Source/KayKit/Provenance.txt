CombatMelee.fbx is imported as Humanoid. The chest and hand mappings are
explicitly corrected for this skeleton. CombatMeleeReference.fbx contains the
same original bytes, imported as Generic, to sample the original weapon slot
orientation and hips rotation without Humanoid retargeting distortion.
These source FBX files are used by the editor baker, not referenced by gameplay.

Generated clips:
  Thrust: Melee_1H_Attack_Stab
  Greatsword1 / Riposte1: Melee_2H_Attack_Slice
  Greatsword2 / Riposte2: Melee_2H_Attack_Stab
  Greatsword3 / AxeChop: Melee_2H_Attack_Chop
  GreatswordReady / AxeReady / SwordGuard: Melee_2H_Idle

Adaptation: WeaponMotionRetargeter retains the source body motion, transfers
hips rotation onto the spine for the game's upper-body mask, fits both hands
to the weapon handles, and retimes the clips and their damage windows.
WeaponLoadout makes a small two-arm reach correction on the visible character
rig at runtime. Trails, hit volumes, and baked server attack tracks use this
same weapon pose. Original character meshes and avatars are unchanged.

Rebuild: Unity menu Battle PvP > Weapons > Install Weapons (Edit Mode only).
The existing Quaternius shield guard and recoil clips remain in use; their
license is retained with the Quaternius source under Remodel/Skills/Source.
