Import / adaptation:
  All nine animation-only downloads use Brute's skeleton. Their importers copy
  the Avatar from Assets/Characters/Brute/Source/Brute.fbx, whose 71 non-root
  bind-pose bones match the downloads. Ch10 is the gameplay retarget destination,
  not the reference skeleton used to import these source files.
  Greatsword attacks, ripostes and sword ready retain the imported
  Humanoid muscle curves. All three greatsword cuts are consecutive portions
  of the original Combo, including its whole-body turn on the third attack.
  Planar root travel is removed; authored body rotation and vertical motion remain.
  A synchronized full-body layer preserves attack footwork. Its duplicate clips
  have no animation events, so damage events only fire from the combat layer.
  Ready-to-attack transitions use an 80 ms Animator crossfade. Hit-window and
  recovery sampling and blade trails follow the incoming attack during that transition.
  Two-handed crosshair calibration is disabled; only the live view correction remains;
  both visible poses and server motion reconstruction use the same calculation.
  Greatsword guard follows live view pitch without residual attack calibration.
  Greatsword attack arms and wrists remain completely as animated. The prop
  follows the two authored fists; the attack does not solve either arm with IK.
  Only the authored static guard fits both hands around their original midpoint.
  SwordGuard is an authored static horizontal hold at the right chest, NOT a
  downloaded sword-parry animation. Axe idle now uses the normal one-handed locomotion pose.
  Static holds retain a standing root pose. Locomotion remains active in ready/guard.
  Shield transitions and thrust use the supplied clips with one-handed adaptation.
  The single-bit axe is Axe(2)_OS from the existing Medieval Melee Weapon Pack;
  its mesh is widened 1.55x and lengthened 1.15x, with matching head hit bounds.
  Axe grip, attack timing and provenance are documented in ../KevinIglesias/Provenance.txt.
  Shield hand and elbow remain behind the shield's panel.
  Gameplay hit events, trails and server attack tracks use the rebuilt poses.
  Trails end at their tracked animation closing phase, never at a generic
  DisableHitBox event (also sent at time zero and by outgoing clips).
